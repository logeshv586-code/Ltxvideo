# Example Prompts

## General Studio

General Studio supports both text-to-video and image-to-video workflows.

### Text → Video

**Prompt:** A small paper boat floats along a quiet stream, the camera slowly tracks beside it as sunlight reflects on the water.

### Image → Video

**Prompt:** A still photo of a cyclist on a quiet road comes alive as the cyclist moves forward, while the camera gently follows from behind.

## Comics Studio

### Text → Video

**Prompt:** A hand-drawn detective walks through a rainy alley, the camera slowly pans from the street to the detective as ink lines and shadows stay consistent.

### Image → Video

**Prompt:** A still comic panel of a cyclist on a hill comes alive as the cyclist rides downhill, while the camera gently zooms in and the illustrated style remains consistent.

## Real World Studio

### Text → Video

**Prompt:** A baker places fresh bread on a wooden counter, the camera slowly moves closer while warm window light falls across the bread.

### Image → Video

**Prompt:** A still photo of a mountain cabin comes alive as tree branches sway gently in the wind, while the camera slowly pans across the cabin.

## Action Studio

### Text → Video

**Prompt:** A runner accelerates along a track, the camera smoothly tracks alongside the runner as the background moves with steady motion.

### Image → Video

**Prompt:** A still image of a skateboarder on a ramp comes alive as the skateboarder rolls forward, while the camera gently follows the movement from the side.

## Cartoon Story Studio

### Text → Video

**Prompt:** A small cartoon fox carries a basket through a forest path, the camera slowly follows from behind while the fox's colors and clothing remain consistent.

### Image → Video

**Prompt:** A still cartoon scene of a child beside a tree comes alive as the child waves to a small bird, while the camera gently pans toward them and the visual style stays consistent.
